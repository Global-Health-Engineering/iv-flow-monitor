# Bench Run V_50_03 — 2026-05-13

Third validation run of the day, executed with a deliberately slower clamp setting after the first V_50_03 attempt landed in the streaming-regime (preserved as `_streaming_`). This run completes the three-point in-rate sweep used to fit and stress-test the scalar correction factor `k` in `analysis/notebooks/validation.ipynb`.

## Anomalies / operator notes

- The first V_50_03 attempt was aborted ~141 s in when the optical chain entered the streaming regime (clamp too open, ~269 mL/hr gravimetric). Data preserved separately at `data/raw/2026-05-13_macro20_50mlh_03_streaming_board1.*` — see `docs/bench/2026-05-13_V_streaming.md`.
- Between attempts the device's drop detector latched into `armed=0` (one photodiode held above `thresh_low` by residual water on the surface). Resolved by reflashing the device (which re-runs the boot baseline cal). The reflashed boot picked up `thresh_T_hi=3919`, `thresh_B_hi=3542` — slightly different from V_50_01/02's calibration but well inside the working envelope.
- Scale stream was running ahead of the UART for ~90 s after the reflash; both were killed and restarted aligned before the canonical V_50_03 capture began.

## Per-drop validation summary

Via `tools/build_bench_overlay.py` on the V_50_03 pair (gravity-corrected mean-of-pulses model, identical physics to the firmware) with `V_CAL_K = 1.27` applied:

| Metric | Value |
|---|---:|
| N drops | 180 |
| Mean V_est (uncalibrated, gravity-corrected) | 45.11 µL |
| Mean V_est (calibrated, × V_CAL_K) | 57.29 µL |
| `k_pre = V_true / V_est_uncal` | **1.315** |
| `k_post = V_true / V_est_cal` | **1.036** (within 4 % of unity) |
| Per-drop CV (uncalibrated) | 4.2 % |

V_50_03 is the **closest-to-calibrated run** in the 2026-05-13 campaign (post-calibration k=1.04). It sits at the mid-envelope flow rate (64.07 mL/h, 59.32 µL/drop) — exactly where `V_CAL_K = 1.27` was designed to fit. The runs at the operating-envelope extremes (V_50_01 large-drops; V_50_02 small-drops at fastest in-envelope rate) drift to k_post = 1.27 and 0.79 respectively.

## Position in the four-run rate sweep

| Run | Gravimetric flow (mL/h) | V_true (µL) | V_est_uncal (µL) | k_pre | k_post |
|---|---:|---:|---:|---:|---:|
| V_50_01 | 93.09 | 73.83 | 45.95 | 1.61 | 1.27 |
| V_50_02 | 83.55 | 39.26 | 39.24 | 1.00 | 0.79 |
| V_50_03 | 64.07 | 59.32 | 45.11 | 1.31 | 1.04 |
| V_50_04 | 44.66 | ≤186.10 | 164.13 | ≥1.13 | ≥0.89 |

The chamber-drop volume varies **inversely** with flow rate over V_50_01..04 (largest drops at slowest clamp, smallest drops at the highest in-envelope rate). This is the surface-tension-vs-drag balance: slow flow lets a drop accumulate at the chamber tip before detaching; fast flow shears drops off early.
